## What the results pages show

- **Assisted stretching:** "assisted stretching bali/uluwatu", "stretch(ing)
  bali" rank the **home page**, not `/uluwatu-bali/assisted-stretching/`. Stretchr
  (site and Instagram) holds the top of "assisted stretching bali".
- **Lymphatic, Uluwatu:** "lymphatic massage uluwatu" is led by service pages
  titled almost like ours ("Lymphatic Drainage Massage | Uluwatu | Bali Bliss
  Spa", "Lymphatic Drainage Massage in Uluwatu — Feel Lighter in an Hour"); our
  page is outside the top 100 for it while ranking 5th for "uluwatu lymphatic
  drainage massage". Watch after the redesign.
- **Lymphatic, Bali:** service pages ("Lymphatic Drainage Massage Bali/Kuta"),
  ClassPass, Fresha and Instagram.
- **Sports massage, Bali:** physio clinics (The Bali Physio, Bodywise),
  Instagram, ClassPass, spa pages ("Sports Massage in Seminyak & Bali").
- **Home massage, Uluwatu:** in-villa services; two put the price in the title
  ("In-Villa Massage IDR 500K", "From Rp 350,000").
- **Cupping massage:** informational (Cleveland Clinic, Wikipedia, NIH). Not a
  title fight for a service page; "cupping near me" (3rd) is the local one.
- **Trauma healing, Bali:** psychological trauma retreats and Indonesian
  content: a different intent from bodywork.
- **Brand:** a different business, "Flex And Flow | Pilates & Yoga Studio"
  (@flexandflow.id, flexandflow.studio), ranks 3rd and 4th for "flex and
  flow". The `WebSite` markup (#3) gives Google our name as "Flex & Flow".
- **People also ask** (FAQ material, not titles): "Are lymphatic massages
  actually worth it?", "Who shouldn't do lymphatic massage?", "How much is a
  1 hour sports massage?", "Is assisted stretching painful?", "How much is a
  massage in Uluwatu?", "Where to get a massage in Uluwatu?".

Ahrefs reports the page's own `<title>`, not the one Google displays, so title
rewrites have to be checked by searching (e.g. `site:flexandflow.fit`).
