## What it is

A small wellness and recovery studio in Uluwatu, Bali. Hands-on bodywork — not a spa, not a
gym. The work is recovery *for people who train*: surfers, athletes, and active residents and
visitors who put their bodies through hard use and need to keep moving.

**Mechanism:** one practitioner, one body, one hour. Sessions are assessed and tailored per
person rather than run off a fixed menu.

## Audience and scene

Uluwatu's surf-and-train population, plus visitors staying nearby. The real scene: someone who
surfed at dawn or trained hard, is stiff or sore, and wants to be able to go again tomorrow.
They decide fast and book on WhatsApp from a phone, often same-day.

## Services (real)

Assisted stretching · sports massage · lymphatic drainage (and a men's detox variant) ·
pregnancy massage · trauma release · cupping therapy · facial massage · outcall/home service.

Each service is priced in two tiers: **Master Therapist** (highly experienced) and
**Therapist** (more affordable). Sessions are typically 60 minutes.
